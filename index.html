KlieneraA business operating system for independent consultants

A consultant's business lives in six disconnected places: a spreadsheet for clients, a notes app for meetings, email for invoices, a payroll sheet, a bookkeeper they can't afford yet. Klienera puts all of it in one workspace, priced in the money people actually earn.

  • Named the product, designed the brand and every screen
  • Built the app: Next.js, Supabase and Postgres with row-level security per business
  • Shipped regional pricing, so local customers pay in taka and international ones in dollars
  • Wired bKash and card payments, with a free plan capped at five clients and Pro unlocking payroll
  • Running it solo: deploy pipeline, customer support, launch

About

03

I build the kind of software I wished existed when I started: fast, affordable, and honest about how small businesses run.

I'm Sadaf Al Selim. I work from Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I design and ship web products on my own: the research, the interface, the database, the deploy and the replies to customer emails.

Klienera is my current focus. It started because freelance studios end up doing bookkeeping at midnight instead of the work they're paid for. So the product covers the boring parts properly: clients, deliverables, meetings, invoices, payroll and books, in one place, in a language and a currency that fits the person paying.

If you're building something and want a product person who can also write the code, that's the conversation I like having.
